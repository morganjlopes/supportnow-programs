Help at the moment of need

Grants

A free database of grants for medical needs. Families filter it down to the grants that fit their situation.

Who it's for

A family facing a medical cost they can't cover on their own.

The problem

Grants exist, but they are scattered and hard to match to one family's situation.

How big

100M Americans affected by medical debt. 250K+ medical GoFundMe campaigns a year.

Today: 3,645 grants listed

grants.supportnow.org · Sources: KFF Health News, GoFundMe

Help at the moment of need

Advocacy Marketplace

Matches families raising children with disabilities to advocates who have been there. Free to get matched. Help is free or affordable.

Who it's for

Parents fighting IEPs, insurance, Medicaid, diagnosis, or the transition at 18. Also the advocates who help them.

The problem

They are learning these systems alone, with no clear person to ask.

How big

7.5M students served under IDEA. That is 15% of public school students.

Today: 56 advocates listed

supportnow.org/advocacy · Source: NCES, school year 2022-23

Help at the moment of need

Funding Coach

Free coaching for treatment, equipment, and major medical costs. We match the family to a coach who has funded something similar.

Who it's for

A family with one big, specific cost: a treatment, a piece of equipment, a wheelchair van.

The problem

They don't know which mix of fundraising, grants, and advocacy will get them there.

How big

250K+ medical GoFundMe campaigns a year, raising $650M+.

supportnow.org/advocacy/apply · Source: GoFundMe via Becker's

Broad reach

Emergency Contact

One printed page for the fridge that tells anyone caring for your kids what they need to know, with a QR code for each person.

Who it's for

Any parent with kids at home, and the sitter or grandparent standing in the kitchen.

The problem

Allergies, doses, doctors, and who can pick up all live in the parent's head.

How big

About 33M US families with children under 18.

[URL] · Source: US Census, 2024

Distribution

Clicklist

The resources a social worker always recommends, on one page a family can keep. Built once, handed over with a QR code.

Who it's for

Hospital social workers and caseworkers. The family just opens it. No sign-up.

The problem

They repeat the same ten links, phone numbers, and addresses to every family, every week.

How big

810,900 US social workers. 193,200 work in healthcare.

supportnow.org/clicklists · Source: BLS, 2024

Broad reach

Support Languages

A free quiz that shows your natural way of helping someone through a major life event. Eight Support Languages, built from three dimensions.

Who it's for

The friend, relative, or coworker of someone in crisis. The supporter, not the family.

The problem

They want to help but don't know what to do, so they often do nothing.

How big

63M family caregivers, 1 in 4 adults. Supporters around them are a bigger group still.

[Screenshot: Support Languages]

[URL] · Source: AARP and NAC, Caregiving in the US 2025

Broad reach

Podcast: Moms Like Me

Jordan interviews moms who have come through major life moments, from disability to diagnosis to loss. They share their stories and advice.

Who it's for

Moms going through something similar right now.

The problem

Isolation. The feeling that nobody around them really gets it.

How big

37.8M moms with kids under 18 at home. 32% of moms listen to podcasts weekly.

Today: about 110 episodes

supportnow.org/podcast · Sources: US Census, Edison Research
